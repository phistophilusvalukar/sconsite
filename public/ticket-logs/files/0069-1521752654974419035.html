Name: Porfirizo Palasseri Ancestry: Human Heritage: Versatile Human Class: Harrow Sorcerer | Thief Rogue Age: 19 Backstory Porfirizo Palasseri, "Poro" to his close friends and family, was born on the road. His parents were part of one of the numerous colourful wagon caravans wandering far and wide across Varisia. Poro was always fascinated with the fortune-tellers in the caravan; the colourful Harrow cards and the stories they told. When Poro came of age, the magic in his blood awoke. He wasn't just fascinated by the Harrow, but had a blood connection to the cards. After discovering that Lepidstadt is an occultist's haven, Poro enrolled at the University of Lepidstadt and developed his sorcerous powers and made coin fortune-telling. One night as he was getting ready for bed, Poro spotted a Harrow card laying on his pillow. The Rabbit Prince. It wasn't one of his though; the artwork was different. As he reached for the strange card… …the world turned inside out and upside down! Porfirizo was whisked away in a Convergence and found himself in a strange land. He found his way to a settlement called Axiom, and some being called The Custodian gave him a peculiar Wayfinder. Apparently parts of different worlds were being converged to this realm called Ao. If things are continuously drawn here, there must be a way back; the connections are still open. Porfirizo has turned to amateur archaeology to unearth secrets that might lead to a way home. (edited)
