LJSmith 10/2/2025 6:44 AM
so my opinion is that it is pretty clear what the RAI is - implements are all inherently objects, just not particularly useful ones in combat. Books, goblets, mirrors, etc. Being an implement isn't the summation of the description of that implement as an object, clearly - its just a quality of that object. You can have a mirror that's not an implement, but you can't have an implement that isn't anything. Implement's Empowerment means you cannot hold a goblet that isn't an implement. It does not mean you are not allowed to hold a Goblet implement because it is also a goblet, and goblets aren't implements - the goblet you are holding is in fact an implement, a Chalice implement, so it does not interfere. There is nothing in the restrictions that says "shields are not allowed", there is nothing that says "if you're holding an object with combat-relevant statistics then that's not allowed", so Shield implement does not confuse anything. The "one-handed weapon" restriction, however, doesn't have any reason to ignore that your Weapon implement is a one-handed weapon. That restriction does not care that your one-handed weapon is an implement or not, it simply allows up to and only one single one-handed weapon to be held. In summary, you can't be holding two one-handed weapons, even if one of them is an implement, because the wording does not imply that all other restrictions are lifted if the object is an implement.
