He's adopted more as a tool than a member as he's used as a living cauldron to brew and test new deadly toxins, and scout dangerous territories of the swamp. He doesn't mind it as he gets to observe and learn the alchemical process. Any successful concoctions are memorized and stored, failures result in blaming the ogre and whacking him with the stinkstick, a stick drenched with the musk of skunks, which he instinctively yet obliviously dodges, and the ones that hit are met with no reaction despite leaving a mark. They have observed him experiment with his own formulas with great success and withstand his own toxins. This quickly concerns the chieftain, as only those who conquer the deadliest toxin get to lead the tribe. Luckily for her, he's only alchemically smart and is easy to convince when venturing onto dangerous missions. Unfortunately for her, he tends to succeed most of the time and the failures leave little to no injuries. Unenthusiastically impressed, the chieftain decides to utilize his skills and sends him on a special mission to find an important ingredient: the elusive Goodbye Hopper, an amphibious beast so toxic that licking or getting licked by it will send you into a different reality. The ogre's mask lights up with joy to have the honor of participating in a sacred quest!
6:20 PM
He searches around behind prickly trees and within the bog waters for days, settling occasionally for a little snack break with whatever creatures he may find nearby... until one day he sees it: a vibrant orange-spotted white frog sitting on top of a mossy rock within a large swamp. He takes a closer look and spots a small, scaled lizard with bulging eyes approaching it. The ogre quickly sprints to stop the scaled monster from devouring the hopper when it suddenly disappears! Looking at where it was and the peacefully croaking frog... he finally found it! With careful precision, he stores the creature in a small jar and happily hops back to his tribe with a chant. The chieftain's daily celebration of the disappeared ogre was short lived as it was disrupted by big, familiar stomps approaching the tribe. She turns around only to look up and see the return of the ogre... everyone stares in awe at the jar holding the sacred frog. The ogre opens it and hands it to the chieftain, who, in a swift rising fury, reveals the quest was a ruse and there was never such a thing. She proves it by licking it only to immediately fall flat on her face. The tribe stare silently... only to cheer as the prophesied leader has finally been revealed! The still oblivious ogre celebrates by holding the frog in front of his tribe and turning it around only for it to lick him... which turns out to be fine! Until a sudden Convergence takes him away, leaving the frog and his tribe behind.
