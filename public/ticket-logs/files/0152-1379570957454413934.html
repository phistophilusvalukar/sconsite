Iron 6/3/2025 3:05 PM
1: There was a long discussion about making this essentially risk free, but we ultimately decided that unlike the resurrection ritual, the reincarnate ritual is something you choose. You have to take a risk and you are choosing to do it. There needs to be a risk; The other option was an exorbitant cost. A risk makes sense in-universe too. Additionally we removed the crit fail condition, which would have been worse. 2: Again, you're choosing to remake yourself. Its a choice. There has to be a cost. This is to enable a higher level character to remake their build. You already get a free remake within your first five games, which is plenty of time to notice a glaring flaw. 3: Its worth noting you're still you. You aren't a different character. You've essentially bodyswapped and some of your early memories are different but you're still you. The reason we're doing this risky and forbidden ritual is because the threat is escalating, and desperate times call for desperate measures. As for why it uses convergences, they are a tool, albeit a dangerous one. We are fighting against uncontrolled convergences, but that doesn't mean the use of controlled convergences is anathema to use. The moral implications are up to your character. Its worth noting the ritual is not meant to be easy, simple, or morally uncomplicated. Unlike the concessions we made with the resurrection ritual, where we determined that death is something that happens to you, and if you want to reverse it you should be able to within reason, this ritual is a choice. If the implications are unappealing to you, you are free not to make that choice, and nothing bad will happen to you.
