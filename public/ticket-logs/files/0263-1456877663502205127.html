You can still use your shield as an implement when it has the broken condition." So...the circumstance bonus reduction of this does absolutely nothing. As soon as a shield falls below 50%, it becomes broken, and literally cannot be used aside from as an implement. You can't actually raise it anymore due to broken condition.
