Name: Fēng Kai (Kai Fēng) Age: 22 Ancestry: Human Heritage: Aiuvarin Classes: Thief Rogue | Wolf Stance Monk Atmospheric Backstory Music: Inazuma by Yu-Peng Chen Backstory: Fēng Kai was born on Golarion in Tian Xia to the fifth concubine of Master Xue Fēng, the renowned master of the Blood Wind Arts. As the youngest among Xue Fēng’s many children, little attention was paid to Kai within the family’s vicious inner-house politics. His mother held little influence among the concubines, and Kai himself was never viewed as an important contender within the family’s struggles for status and succession. As a result, much of his youth was spent isolated from the schemes of the household, leaving him alone with little more than martial training and discipline to occupy his time. Everything changed when he met his older half-brother, Zhào. Kai barely remembers how their first meeting truly began, only that it almost immediately devolved into a fight. The two boys brawled so fiercely that they crashed into an ancient ceremonial pot and shattered it, earning both of them a harsh scolding from their father. From that day onward, however, the two became inseparable. Their rivalry differed from the others within the family. Both silently understood that the politics of the house were a losing game and instead created competitions of their own. First it was who could eat more. Then who could charm the attention of the local jade beauties. Then who was stronger. Over time, the rivalry forged both of them into skilled martial artists, each sharpening themselves against the other. Zhào excelled in deception, social manipulation, and unpredictable trickery. Much to Kai’s silent envy—an envy he would never openly admit—his brother always seemed naturally gifted in those arts. Realizing he could never rival Zhào in cunning, Kai instead focused on what came naturally to him: directness, honesty, discipline, and relentless martial training. If the two fought directly, Kai would often overpower his brother through sheer ferocity and endurance. Zhào, however, always found ways to twist situations in his favor through cleverness, speed, or outright trickery. As the years passed, their rivalry matured into genuine teamwork. They came to understand one another so thoroughly that they fought like two halves of the same beast, naturally compensating for each other’s weaknesses. Their father eventually began dispatching them together on errands, deliveries, and more dangerous assignments, where the brothers proved remarkably effective as a pair. Then Godsrain happened. Gorum’s death sent shockwaves across all of Golarion, reaching even Tian Xia. Kai watched in equal awe and horror as the god fell from the heavens. Amid the chaos, one of Gorum’s falling shards of divine light struck Kai directly, grievously wounding him. Zhào dragged his brother’s broken body away from the disaster, desperately trying to return home. Before they could make it back, however, a supernatural storm swept across the land. It swallowed both brothers whole, transporting them far away from everything they had ever known. In the process, Kai’s wound was mysteriously healed.
