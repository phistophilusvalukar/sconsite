Name: Damian Rottguard Ancestry: Human Heritage: Skilled Human (Society) Class: Flurry Ranger | Mastermind Rogue Age: 21 Backstory: Damian Rottguard was born in Golarion in the city of Opparos to a noble family, the Rottguards. They were a minor house in the city, but a fairly wealthy one. They owned a large wine vineyard and production facility, and they made good money from it. Damian was the first born of the Rottguards, so he was the shining son of his family. His father, Augustus Rottguard spared no expense with his son’s education. Damian received the best tutors and swordmasters, and he took well to the teaching. Well, anyone would take well to it with the money Augustus was spending on tutorage. Of all the teachings Damian took to, he enjoyed his sword training the most, and, as he grew older, he started in the dueling circuit at Opparos, doing very well there. Occasionally, he would visit other towns to establish wine trades, but, in truth, it was mostly to duel other people and learn other sword arts. Eventually, people stopped dueling him. The other nobility would purposefully avoid dueling him in the circuits due to the risk of losing. Few nobility were as obsessed as Damian with their training. Damian grew frustrated with dueling the same opponents and took matters into his own hands. He became somewhat of a scoundrel. He would insult or coerce the other nobility into dueling him. These would progress into greater insults and more bolder accusations from him as he goaded more people into duels. However, all of this came to a head when, at a masquerade ball, he became romantically involved with the betrothed of another guests. She was the betrothed of Octavius Stavian, one of the sons of the royal family. But, instead of being goaded into a duel, Octavius took a different approach. Octavius hired The Golden Hand, a band of ruthless mercenaries, to take out Damian. A few weeks after the ball, they besieged the Rottguard estate. But Damian was not there. He was out on the town drinking and carousing. He only arrived home the next morning, still drunk, to a smoldering ruin. He was devastated, his world gone, and, while his family faced this, he was out carousing. He was not stupid, he knew Octavius was behind this, for he still hadn't accepted Damian's duel, but Damian was reckless. The next day, he stormed into the royal palace, killing several guards as he entered. There, he demanded a duel. Octavius was shocked and surprised, but he knew Damian’s judgment was clouded in this moment. He told Damian that he would accept a duel, on the stipulation that he could bring two friends to help him. Damian accepted. He knew it was stupid, but he didn’t care, and the rage he felt made it impossible to refuse. Their duel began the next day with much of Opparos nobility present. The duel was brutal, Damian held his own for a while against 3 people, and they seemed somewhat matched. But, Octavius did not play fair. He used poison on his blade, and, as the match progressed, Damian’s defeat became inevitable. He lost, collapsing after everything became too much. Some of those witnessing wanted to stand against the rigged fight, but many were bullied by Damian before or were afraid of getting on Octavius’ bad side, so no one spoke up. Instead of being slain in the arena, Damian was left in a cell. There, he would slowly succumb to the poison and his wounds. But, as he faded in and out of awareness, a voice came to him, a spirit’s voice. It spoke of revenge against the ones who destroyed Damian’s family, and against Octavius. It wanted its own revenge against The Golden Hand, and it saw Damian as a vessel for this. It showed him how to harness his spirit and how to allow it to help him. Damian followed its teachings, and as he perished, he willed himself to stay in the realm of the living.

Damian’s body was taken to a mass grave in the city, but that night, he awoke. Well, only partially. Through the spirit’s teachings, it possessed his body, merging with him. The next few months were a haze to Damian as he was both dead and not dead, conscious and unconscious. The spirit used its own strange magics and Damian’s physical skill to track down and eliminate The Golden Hand. After its task was done, it thanked him and vanished into the beyond, its own emotional tether to the world was gone, so it moved on, and Damian was left alone. He had his fill of revenge as well, but he had a new problem. His soul and body were unraveling. He was meant to die in the duel, but he was brought back, and there were consequences. He began a journey to find more of those spirits or people knowledgeable in these things who could help him fix his soul. Eventually, he felt a strange sensation, he assumed it was a disturbance in the realm of spirits. Hoping it was one of the spirits he spoke with before, he followed the sensation to a cave, and through that cave, he came to Ao. Now he seeks to find a cure for his condition in this place and find a way to continue the legacy of the Rottguard. (edited)
