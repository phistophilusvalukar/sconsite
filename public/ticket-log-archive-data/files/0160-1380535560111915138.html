This isn't of urgency, but before the level cap gets bumped up you may want to reconsider the Duelling Dance houserule. These are the concerns:
  • Extravagant Parry is much stronger than Duelling Parry, and Duelling Dance may have been intentionally left out of the remaster to balance this
  • Duelling Dance has the same prerequisites as Duelling Parry (must be wielding a one-handed melee weapon and have your other hand completely free), so while it functions with Extravagant Parry as per this server's houserule it is extremely limited compared to Extravagant Parry (must be wielding at least one one-handed melee weapon)
I'd suggest one of the outcomes:
  • Note in the rules that the requirements for the Duelling Dance stance are still limited compared to Extravagant Parry
  • Match Duelling Dance's restrictions with Extravagant Parry
  • Remove the houserule entirely Any of these outcomes would reduce confusion a fair bit - I know at least a couple of people didn't realise that the stance requirements were a thing they'd need to work around
