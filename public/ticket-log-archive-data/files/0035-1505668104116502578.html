Name: Siv Ulriksdóttir Ancestry: Kitsune Heritage: Frozen Wind Kitsune Class: Oscillating Wave Psychic (Gathered Lore) | Outwit Ranger Age: 24 Backstory Siv Ulriksdóttir's tailless form has a tail. As a kitsune, this makes her somewhat of an oddity. But there's a perfectly reasonable explanation for this. It just needs a bit of context. So let us begin at the beginning. Long ago, a group of frozen wind kitsune endeavored to chart an expedition across the Crown of the World itself, hiking from Tian Xia all the way to Avistan, loosely along the Path of Aganhei. To their surprise, they handily succeeded, for the most part. They did, however, stray a bit too far from the path in the Rimethirst Mountains, and were hunted by giants no longer contract-bound to leave them be. This deviation from their route led them to a settlement they did not know existed, a community of fiendish nephilim well-suited to the chill of the peaks, who hid them away and sheltered them from the giants. These nephilim were all runaways for various reasons - persecution in Cheliax, forced conscription in the Lands of the Linnorm Kings, and an infestation of demons in Sarkoris numbering among them. The kitsune knew what it was like living secretively away from a world that wouldn't care to understand their way of life. So it was the two communities bonded, and after publishing an account of most of their journey down the Path of Aganhei, omitting information about the nephilim of course, the kitsune decided to return and live alongside the nephilim, joining their two families and using their innate magic to help support their way of life. Atop the nearby mountain, they built a shrine in thanks to their gods Daikitsu and Yamatsumi. It was into this community that Siv was born, and given a name in honor of the leader of the Thanelandic nephilim that saved her people, Ulrik. As many kitsune tend to mimic the type of folk they grow up around with their tailless form, it felt natural for her to take on the appearance of a nephilim to better fit in with friends and family. She came to be proud of the form, as it represented people she looked up to and cherished. In fact, Siv felt and still feels more comfortable in her tailless form than in her native kitsune fur, identifying more with it than the foxlike appearance of her blood relatives. She valued her people's culture and traditions as much as the nephilims’, this was simply what felt right. In spite of the harsh and unforgiving environment she grew up in, Siv had a happy childhood, with plenty of time to find herself and discover her passions. And there was nothing Siv was more passionate about than monsters. From the moment Siv caught her first glimpse of a yeti, she fell in love with the idea of finding out everything about it. What it ate, how it hunted, where it lived, how it raised children… she needed to decipher the mystery of this new creature. And when she found out there were more strange monsters around her home, she had to know about them too. This thirst for knowledge quickly took on a different bent when she realized what she knew could help her people hunt the creatures around their home and secure food and livelihood for their community. She practiced every day with the bow, and studied by candlelight into the night, learning which vital spots to hit for a quick, clean kill without spoiling the meat. And she was good at it. Siv's knowledge made her useful to her village, and she found she loved feeling useful. (2/?)

` Joining the village's hunters, she had the privilege of partaking in a longstanding tradition maintained by the nephilim of the village, predating the arrival of the kitsune: raising a riding drake. The sturdy grip of the talons and the speed at which they could navigate the mountain trails made the drakes ideal mounts and companions for the hunting parties of her village, and the fact that they could always start a fire if they brought wood didn't hurt either. She named her young drake Skald after both the language and the poets, as he always had a bit of a contemplative look about him, like a scaly poet pondering his next verse. She loves him with all her heart, and to her, he's as much family as any beloved pet can be. When raising Skald, Siv started to feel a sort of connection to the inner fire of her companion, feeling kinship to him as she had the same flame in her own heart, steady growing. It was during a deadly run-in with an ice troll that Siv first discovered she could project that flame outward, her desperation propelling it forth to burn the creature and stop its regeneration. She found that the combination of her inner fire and her connection to the cold from the environment she'd been raised in and her bloodline allowed her to manipulate a sort of balance, using the strength of her will to bend flame and frost to her whims. Over the years, Siv worked to nurture this power inside of her, and found it extended beyond simple temperature manipulation. Her mind was exceptional, and she could use it to other ends too, like healing the injured when a hunt went south. As it turned out, this made Siv even more useful to her people, which in turn made her even happier and even more motivated. She decided she didn't need any of the complexities of modern life down there in the rest of Golarion. No, she had everything she needed right here with her people, and life was good. (3/?)
1:30 PM
Unfortunately, the good just couldn't last. Years later, her hunting party had another run in with an ice troll, this one of a horrifying two-headed variety and twice as deadly. She prepared to wield her flames in their defense, but in a terrible turn of events, that was her moment of Convergence. To her horror, she had been forcibly taken from her people at the moment they needed her. Now, Siv needs to find a way to navigate this new world she never thought she'd have to, adjust to city life, and find a way home to check on her people. She has to believe they survived, but the possibility she failed them haunts her… (4/4)
