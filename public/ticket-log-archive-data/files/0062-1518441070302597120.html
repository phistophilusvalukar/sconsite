Name: Fidget Ancestry: Gnome Heritage: Otter Beastkin Class: Inventor/Champion Age: 24 Backstory: Fidget the otter-person was found near a small, nondescript village in the middle of nowhere. There were no signs of her parents or other family. She was taken in by the town librarian since most of this town wanted nothing to do with this random beast child. The librarian had a daughter of about the same age. The girl was frail and sickly and would often be bedridden until she lost the ability to walk altogether. This was somewhat offset by her psychic abilities. Fidget would be the one to craft the girl's wheelchair, although not without being told to not add in a bunch of "innovations." Fidget, having grown up in a library, loved to read. Her favorite stories were about heroes. Selfless champions who would do anything to protect others, no matter the personal cost. Her favorite story was about Acavna, the hero who sacrificed herself to move the moon in the path of a falling meteor, saving the entire planet from destruction at the cost of her life. Fidget had vowed to be the girl's hero, and soon, she would have the chance to become a full-fledged hero in her own right.
7:29 PM
One day, the small town was besieged by the undead. Fidget, the girl, and a few others defended the village and fought off the undead. But soon after, people all over town started getting sick, a plague had begun spreading. Fidget and the girl left the town in search of help and a cure. Along the way, they found a strange building. Inside were scientific wonders Fidget could only have dreamed about. Computers and machines, flying robotic drones that shot lasers at the group and other such marvels. Fidget and her friends smashed the drones and then... They exploded. Fidget remembers pushing the girl's wheelchair away to save her, but got caught in the blast. She accepted this fate, knowing that she had at least saved the girl's life.
7:30 PM
Fidget was... Somewhere else. She knew nothing of this town but had no choice but to begin life anew there, alone. She set up a lab in the nearby forest where she got to work trying to create technology like she'd seen in that building. She was obsessed and eventually she created her power armor. Her joy was short lived as soon after, her new home was once again set upon by the undead. Fidget fought off the undead, but, of course, the plague had begun spreading through her new home again. Fidget had no choice but to go find people who could help her. Once again, she never made it to a cure. While traveling, she heard the girl's voice in her head. The girl who had become a distant memory... Fidget had even forgotten her name... A psychic rift, a loved one trying desperately to bring her back... But once again, Fidget found herself in a mysterious place, meeting a mysterious.... Person? At least she thinks it was a person? It merely pointed and Fidget found a place once again technologically fascinating beyond anything she had seen. And this is where Fidget's newest journey would begin, on Ao, in Axiom. She knows this time she can succeed and become a true hero!
