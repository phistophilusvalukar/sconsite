Name: Bramble Timbertwine Ancestry: Yaksha Heritage: Dromaar Class: Ligneous Instinct Barbarian | Monk Age: 127 Backstory Bramble's existence began with the unfortunate occurrence of her dromaar mother going into labor while out in the wilds of the Ironbound Archipelago gathering berries, then accidentally stumbling through a gateway to the First World. Being born into the realm of the fey resulted in some… alterations. As such, rather than the human dromaar child she had been expecting, Bramble's mother instead wound up giving birth to a yaksha dromaar. Now trapped in the Evergrove, her mother tried to make the best of their situation, but, unbeknownst to Bramble as she was too young to remember, the fey quickly claimed her child as their own, and in a small mercy, whisked her mother off back home. To begin with, Bramble never knew her real mother and father. Bramble was raised in the style of the fey by her two faerie mothers, a dryad and a hesperid who lived together in a hollow tree on a hill overlooking the strange sunsets of the First World. From a young age, Bramble wondered why she looked so different from her parents, but they always dodged the question when it came up. They taught her the importance of being a guardian of natural places, of the beauty of wilds preserved, of the wondrous properties of all the local flora. It was from them that she learned the art of herbalism, as the duo peddled cures to locals who took ill. It was a happy childhood. But it was not to last, but to be tainted… When Bramble learned the truth about her blood mother, told to her when she came of age, she was horrified. Her fey mothers tried to explain to her that due to her nature, she would not have felt at home in Golarion, and she would have been eternally unsatisfied and out-of-place. She was having none of it. She promptly packed her things in spite of the protestations of her guardians, and she ran away that very same night... (char. limit TBC)

Bramble didn't know where she could possibly go, so she just kept walking and walking. She managed to sustain herself off the land for many years. But for the better part of a decade, eight long years, she kept walking. Those eight years led her to a critical mistake she would live to regret. She had eventually reached a river, and thought to take a boat somewhere, anywhere else. Just further away. But she failed to pay the proper respects owed to Ragadahn. She was caught by one of Ragadahn's crueler subordinates, a rusalka who went by the moniker Reedsnarl. For her transgressions, invested by the power of Ragadahn, the rusalka subjected her to a terrible fate. Bramble was forced into a pact of one-thousand years of servitude, a twist of her guardian spirit nature where she was made to protect the mouth of a cave along the river where some of Ragadahn's primordial secrets were kept. For a century, she remained there. After decades, the thought that her blood mother would be long-dead of old age and that she would never get the chance to meet her weighed heavily on Bramble. Heavier still was the burden of the lives she took, the lives of curious wanderers that may have been good-hearted but made the same mistake she did, the mistake of trespassing in ignorance. Her rusalka captor placed their severed heads on stakes around the cave entrance, forcing Bramble to constantly live around reminders of what she had been made to do. (2/3)
