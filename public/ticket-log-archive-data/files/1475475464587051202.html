Ærindël'nafthiëllé Edasseril

Ærindël'nafthiëllé Edasseril was the crown princess of the elves, hailing from the ancient Kingdom of Kyonin. Long before the Thassilonian Empire was even a thought, she crossed paths with a young, visionary human traveler named Xin. Captivated by his ambitions and potential, she accompanied him on his early journeys. To the brilliant but mortal Xin, her complex elven name was a mouthful, so he affectionately dubbed her "Naaft." When Xin eventually forged his empire, he did not forget his companion. He granted her rule over the verdant Peridot Isle and its surrounding lands, naming the territory "Edasseril" in honor of her royal family and her beauty. As she invited her elven kin to help build a utopia of shared art, magic, and craftsmanship, her human subjects—untrained in the flowing elven tongue—adopted Xin's pet name for her. Patient, kind, and deeply charitable, the princess accepted being called "Naaft" without complaint, perfectly content to foster a bond with the humans she sought to elevate. History, written by the short-lived and short-sighted, would later warp this truth. The scholars of Golarion mistakenly recorded Naaft as a bitter human who stole an elven princess’s name out of sheer, covetous spite. The irony is tragic, for in those early days, Naaft truly embodied the virtue of Charity. But her human subjects lacked the elven perspective of time. Her grand, well-meaning bureaucracy slowly crumbled into corruption as petty officials scrambled for short-term gains. Deeply disillusioned by this mortal greed, she abdicated her throne long before the empire's descent into sin. A thousand years later, when Earthfall loomed and the elves retreated to Sovyrian, Naaft refused to flee. Her heart broken long ago by the assassination of Xin, she chose to wait for the end in the realm that still bore her name. But the apocalypse brought a different kind of doom to Edasseril. The upstart runelord Belimarius—who was currently sitting on Naaft’s former throne—triggered a catastrophic malfunction in her runewell, threatening to shatter the region's place in time itself. Unwilling to let her legacy end in a tear in reality, Naaft stepped into the breach. She wove a massive matrix of abjuration magic to contain the runewell’s fallout, bracing herself against the raw arcane backlash. She held that ward for ten thousand years. Over the millennia, her physical elven form burned away, gradually fusing with the very abjuration magic she was channeling until she became a living spell—an entity of pure, sustained warding magic. In the crushing, silent isolation of her vigil, her mind began to warp. The benevolent tenets of her goddess, Lissala, twisted from dutiful charity into harsh, demanding obedience. Naaft’s own spirit mirrored this divine rot. The boundless charity that defined her early rule curdled into a deep, agonizing Envy. She did not covet wealth or petty titles; she envied time. She envied the freedom of mortals who could rest, who had the agency to live their lives, and whose good deeds were not rewarded with a ten-millennium prison sentence. When outside forces finally shattered the runewell’s magic and restored Edasseril to the modern timeline, Naaft’s eternal watch ended. But she was not granted peace. In the chaotic instant the runewell collapsed, the violently unraveling abjuration magic collided with a mysterious, external cosmic anomaly. This sudden, unpredictable tear in reality gripped her fragile magical essence and violently ripped her from Golarion entirely. Her ancient, vast power was fractured and burned out in the transit. Now, stranded in an unfamiliar world by sheer cosmic accident, Naaft must rebuild her shattered strength, driven by the bitter conviction that the universe still owes her a monumental debt. ---- ‎
