Ever since I was a hatchling… I’ve had an interest in magics of all types A green dragon wyrmking, freshly hatched looks up her parents… then at a gold dragon priest of Gaasham weaving divine magic to clean her and welcome her into the world I have always had an interest in the many magical forces that underlies everything in existence A young green dragon only a few years old has many religious texts open, books decorated to gods such as Apsu, Brixori, Otilaz and even the forbidden gods such as Dahak. When I approached adulthood, I was given a choice of which path to walk. Study the arcane, immerse myself in the occult, learn from the old natural ways or beseech the divine. They told me I can only choose one, that it is not wise to stretch myself thin across numerous studies. That I can’t wield everything… The dragon looks up and smirks Well, can’t I? Days and nights go by as the dragon studies numerous texts of various magics… the dragon picks up scrolls and books and tosses it across the room in anger Why settle for one path… when I can fly overhead and ignore the idea of a ‘singular focus’… I studied as a wizard and I had natural primal magic as a green dragon… but the occult and divine was just out of my grasp for divine power is not something you can just study and get like the arcane. I wasn’t gifted by an otherworldly source like sorcerers, oracles or clerics nor was I gifted with occult power like psychics… and I am no bard, you wouldn’t catch me dead playing a violin Panicked shouts and roaring is heard as intense energy surrounded her home … then the convergence happened… At the edge of Pyrah, the green dragon reached out a claw to touch the essence that was transporting her world …and I found what I was looking for The essence clings onto her, and wraps itself around her. She roars in pain as coalesced magic of various types unmerge onto her I should have died. It was a miracle I didn’t. But when I woke up… my mind was expanded, I had become a psychic. Furthermore, I’ve been gifted with a curse of divine origin. I was an oracle… was it the convergence that gifted me this power I so sought, or had the convergence allowed me to commune with Yluma, so she may give me what I desired… it matters not, I have what I deserved Magic coalesces around a now young adult green dragon. A divine glow surrounds her head, she wears a hood of various holy symbols, her tail is wrapped in primal infused vines, psychic energy creates an illusionary third eye as her eyes glow with occult power and arcane energy weaves between her talons I am Cyfrin… I wield EVERY magic, and my research has only just begun
