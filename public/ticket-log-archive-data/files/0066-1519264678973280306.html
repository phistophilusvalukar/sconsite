Name: Tesna Ancestry: Minotaur Heritage: Dwarf Class: Inventor | Fighter Age: 28 Backstory The brisk town of Snowford, in the foothills of the Mindspin Mountains, was the place young Tesna called home. Living with her mother, she was a foolhardy, inquisitive sort, always fascinated with the workings of things, utterly convinced that she could make improvements. However, people don't seem to want improvements, she found, so she on more than one occasion, decided to provide her services, and ask for forgiveness rather than permission. This often led to her being chased from the homes of neighbours when they came home before she'd managed to put things right again, but their neighbor Phori was rather pleased with her arm-powered clothes spinner ('In this weather, wool dries too slowly!' she had told her friends) On Tesna's fourteenth birthday, her mother, seeing a spirit that wouldn't be quenched, surprised her with a set of her own blacksmith's tools. This, along with the makeshift forge Tess had set up out the backyard, was everything she needed. She hugged her mum, and was out the door in a flash, her mind whirring with ideas

Years passed, and Tesna slowly worked her craft, till she'd outgrown the little town she called home. As her mother saw her horns disappear over the horizon, she hoped she would one day see her again She could scarcely believe her eyes. The towering stone face carved into the mountain ahead of her, hundreds of feet tall, unblinking eyes seeming to stare at her as she approached the dwarven city of Highhelm, home to the greatest smithing guild in the land, if what she'd heard was true. In her hand she held a letter of recommendation, from the smith in her village. He'd once known a guild metalsmith and spoke of him fondly. If he still lived, the Snowford smith's recommendation would buy her consideration, an audience at least. As she reached the gates, and the sentry stepped forth, she clutched the letter tighter. Guild life was everything she'd hoped for, and less. The money was coming in, and in the last two years, she'd worked with materials she'd never seen before, seen curved blades forged well over her own height, seen elven chain so delicate and so quiet, it flowed like cloth. It would be perfect, but for Revhor Shallowstone. The guildsmith had spoken against her being granted full guild status, despite her obvious talent. In the last two years, she had been ridiculed and attacked, and on no more than three occasions, she'd found him to have cited guild rules written over a century ago, trying fervently to get her removed. Despite this, she had a house (Small as it was, no amount of renovation could raise the roof another three feet), and she had made many friends both within the guild, and within her district. The city was always moving, and there was always work to be done, she had no regrets. She had one regret. While arguing, and in a fit of anger, she had accused Revhor of underhanded tactics, something she had heard spoken of in hushed tones, from apprentices and merchants. Bribes, sabotage, she'd only heard rumors, clearly, she would never accuse him of it to his face, except. Well, she just had. She stormed off as his face turned red, and somehow knew this was going to come back to bite her. Two months passed, and things seemed to quiet down. Revhor hadn't brought up the outburst, he barely seemed to notice her anymore. That was good. Tesna had come home early, finishing her work sooner than expected, when she noticed that her door was unlocked. She always locked it, was it possible she forgot? Stepping inside, she paused, as she noticed the glint of a blade, sitting on the worktable. It was unfinished, but it was unmistakably a clan knife.

It wasn't a threat, or even an invitation. This was evidence Evidence she'd been forging a clan dagger. Evidence she was attempting to claim clanship to get ahead in the guild. Planted evidence, by whoever unlocked her door.
