Eidolon's Name - The Winding Path, aka. "Spiky" Type - Psychopomp (STR primary) Age - Around 500 Backstory The Winding Path has been known by many names over the centuries he's fullfilled his job as a sheperd of souls, this one was the latest. He picked it up based on the local stories and tales told by the mortals. Lately, he's been drawn to the area of a settlment the mortals called Tral Fort. There've been many stray souls in and around the city to sheperd, and the constant expansion and infights of the area have provided him with plenty work. One organization in particular he needed to pay extra attention was the local "criminals". He never paid much mind to morals of the material plane, or wether this particular group would need judgment. It will come to them eventually, and he wasn't the one to deliver it. He tended to follow around the agents of this community around in the shadows and in the fringes between the planes. One particular instance, he was nearby a small dragonet and a dwarf battling an elven mage. He just stood by, but his instincts were telling him something was wrong about this. He initially dismissed, there was little the battle of the mortals could do to him, he was there to guide the ones who would perish. As an explosion knocked both parties down, he moved closer, but as he did, reality on all planes rippled, and broke. It caught him off-guard, and the sheer amount of magic present overloaded his senses, a weird sensation. Once his vision cleared, he found himself on the Material Plane, in his physical form, with the small dragonet laying nearby. He would've left and shifted back to the Boneyard, but something kept him there. A weird connection to the dragonet. He would need to see what this might be, but first and foremost, he needed to deposit him in a safe place. (edited)
